Research interests

Areas of work

Instrumentation, embedded platforms and intelligent processing across aerospace, biomedical and critical-infrastructure applications.

01 / Navigation

Inertial navigation & sensor fusion

Strap-down INS/MAGNETO/GPS fusion on MEMS sensors for attitude estimation and high-precision global positioning of airborne and autonomous platforms.

02 / Biosignals

Biomedical signals & monitoring

EEG and physiological-signal processing, biosensors, machine-learning analysis and real-time biomonitoring platforms.

03 / Integrated systems

Embedded & interoperable systems

Embedded data-acquisition platforms, HL7 FHIR / IoMT interoperability, and electronic systems for critical infrastructures.

04 / Metrology

Measurement & instrumentation

Measurement systems, virtual instrumentation, sensor interfaces and signal conditioning for industrial and medical applications.
